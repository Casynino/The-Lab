# Storylines — ZELab rep guide (login page video)

Recommended: **A**, because one real order worked through start to finish is the clearest way for a
new rep to see every step, in the app's own screens.

## A. One order, start to finish (built)
Device: the worked example. Form: object-to-object chain — every tap becomes the next screen.
The Lab on the Dar coast (the sign-in film's own world) shrinks into a phone's screen; then one sample
order of 10 boxes travels through the real app: log in, Request, approved, Settle, wallet, Withdraw,
Return, More time. A rolling one-word chapter title above the phone says where we are.
First 2 s: the Lab world full-frame, "From the Lab / to your wallet" landing word by word.
Beats: world → phone → Log in → Request → Approved → Settle → Wallet → Withdraw (payoff) → Return →
More time → "Request. Settle. Get paid." → ZELab mark.
Look: the app's own night sky (near-black, red haze, stars), brand red #ee3124 rationed, Inter.
Sound: taps, quick whooshes on lifts, one bass hit on the payoff; no music bed (no music source here).

## B. Juma's week
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take.
Monday 08:00 a request, Tuesday the boxes, Thursday the money: time stamps carry a rep through his
week with the phone in hand.

## C. The box's journey (bold)
Device: one object across worlds. Form: a flight through one world.
One box leaves the Lab, rides to Arusha, is sold, and the money flies back as commission; the camera
follows the box, and the phone shows each step as it happens.

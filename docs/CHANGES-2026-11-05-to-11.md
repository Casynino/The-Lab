# ZELab (The Lab) — what changed, 5–11 November 2026

Context for anyone picking this up: ZELab (www.zelab.site) is the stock, settlement and commission system
for a Tanzanian rolling-paper distributor (brands OHIS and Civlily). Sales reps request stock, get it
issued as a 72-hour order, settle it box by box (paying OHIS to M-Pesa and Civlily to Airtel Money),
return what they cannot sell, and earn commission per settled box. The owner approves everything.
Stack: React + Vite client, Express + Prisma (PostgreSQL on Neon), deployed on Vercel. 156 changes this
week, all live unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Brand and sign-in
- Rebranded from Hao Labs / "The Lab" to **ZELab**: official logo everywhere (sign-in, icons, share card,
  PDFs, QR page), new domain www.zelab.site; old Vercel addresses redirect (cron paths excluded).
- Sign-in page is one moving 3D film of ZELab's corner of Tanzania (the Lab on the Dar coast, port, roads,
  lorries, people). "Log in" is plain text with a red line; the form opens as a small frosted card with
  the email field focused so saved passwords appear at once.
- Installable full-screen app on any phone; "Get the app" pop-up shows only right after a log-in, once,
  and never again after it is closed (the sidebar button still opens it).
- Idle sign-out after 1 hour, silently; pop-ups and toasts keep clear of the phone's notch and home bar.
- **"How it works" video** on the sign-in page (see 8).

## 2. Admin shell and dashboard
- Glass sidebar (from Target Express) that folds to an icon rail; one-word menu items; no logo at the top
  of the sidebar; tapping your name opens your Profile.
- Banner "Mambo vipi? {name}"; dashboard with the shape of the business, today's money, and a small
  scrolling "Attention" box; admin layout fits a phone.

## 3. Reps' cities (regions)
- A rep can have one or many of Tanzania's 31 regions (first = main; blank = Dar es Salaam). Admin adds
  them when creating the rep or later from the rep's profile, Edit — **no limit** (was 5).
- **New request:** a one-city rep sees his city already chosen. A rep with several cities uses the city
  words as tabs — boxes set under a city are that city's, each tab shows its count, "+3 Arusha" shows what
  is set elsewhere; Request without a city says "Choose the city".
- Every request line saves its city (new optional DB columns `stock_requests.region` and
  `stock_request_items.region`, additive migrations, already applied in production). On approval, lines of
  the same product are issued as one stock line, so orders, the 72 h clock and commission are unchanged.
  Requests, admin approvals and the owner's WhatsApp show the city per line.
- Reports still count a multi-city rep's sales under his main city (open question).

## 4. Rep app redesign (premium, plain, fewer words)
- App-wide soft galaxy background; active items as soft glass cards; finished items as plain lists that
  open and close; one pill-shaped action bar in every window (Cancel + one word: Request, Settle, Return,
  Extend).
- **Home:** wallet and bonus cards, four equal tiles (Request, Settle, Report, Return), open orders with
  the most urgent first (3 at most, "See all").
- **Wallet** (was "Commissions" for reps; Wallet is a tab in the bottom bar): wallet card (balance,
  Withdraw, hide amounts, earned / paid out / pending), tabs Overview (My earnings with Earned / Boxes /
  Best month, settlement record, brands, Best sellers), Bonus (one small ring per bonus), Payouts, Fines,
  Rules (rates, fines table, three short rule lines).
- **Requests / Settle / Returns:** New request / New return action cards, active items as cards, History.
- **Order screen:** deadline and time left only (red with the fine when overdue), boxes as a ring,
  per-product bars, details folded; **More time** window (now -> new deadline, fines normally vs after).
- **Profile:** name with a small blue verified badge, rep number, cities as plain text, the wallet card,
  "How you are doing", activity cards, closed orders, Change password (only here).
- **Confirmations:** "Deposited" and "Withdrawal successful" are small and calm, with "You'll get a
  WhatsApp once it's approved." **Toasts:** clear solid cards with short words app-wide.
- Notifications page grouped by day.

## 5. Orders, settlements and returns
- Settle several products at once (grouped by brand, each with its own amount and payment account; saved
  all or none; one notification and one WhatsApp to the owner).
- Simpler settle and return forms (steppers, "All" shortcut, readable payment numbers), numbered product
  lists, order and request pop-ups titled with just the number.

## 6. WhatsApp and reports
- All WhatsApp texts in one clean format (bold title, Label: value lines, no emoji, groups separated by a
  blank line); stock-request lines lettered (a) (b) (c); the city shown.
- Daily, weekly and monthly reports are designed PDFs (letterhead, period comparison, profit and loss,
  money in and out, charts, brands, products, best rep, stock, accounts, attention).
- **Yearly report** (11 Nov): on 1 January, after December's monthly report, the owner gets the whole year
  — sales and net profit month by month (a loss drawn below the line), best / weakest month, months in
  loss, quarters, then the usual sections; archived as "Yearly". Months before the accounts start are dashes.
- Weekly report moved to **Tuesday 08:00 EAT** (the Monday-to-Sunday week before). A report whose run
  failed is sent late by the morning check; failed WhatsApp messages can be retried from Settings.
- Every report (daily included) is listed in Profile → Reports and Finance → Statements, a page at a time.
- CallMeBot refuses some messages with a www.zelab.site link (403): such a message is re-sent at once
  without the link, pointing to Profile → Reports.

## 7. Fixes
- Rep Profile "Could not load activity data": a missing export (since 1 Aug) — fixed, with a test that
  guards every commission-service export.
- Safe areas for the installed app; sign-in and install-sheet polish; packaging die-lines (70x36 / 77x44)
  and the Pepa QR product page.

## 8. "How it works" video
- 55-second portrait film made with HyperFrames (project in `docs/video/rep-guide`): logo intro over the
  moving Lab film, then one sample order through the real app screens — log in, request, approved,
  settle, wallet, withdraw, return, more time — music (generated locally with MusicGen), tap sounds,
  whooshes. Real screens with sample data only. 3.2 MB on the sign-in page, downloaded only when tapped.

## 9. Audit and fixes (11 Nov)
- Full production-readiness audit on a local copy of the live data (writes never touched production),
  then two reviewed fix rounds across stock requests, orders and settlements, returns and fines, the
  wallet, finance, reports and schedules, and the admin screens. The owner's report is kept privately.
- Sales reps may log in **only from a mobile phone** (admin from any device), enforced on the server.
- Admin pages use the rep side's colour mix: soft neutral glass, colour on the figures, red only for the
  banner, main buttons, alerts and the one card that needs action.
- Rules made explicit: **low stock is measured in the warehouse** (boxes out with reps no longer hide a
  product that needs reordering); a warehouse cash sale cannot be paid above its total; the owner's own
  money shows as "Your money in" / "Profit taken out" in the ledger.
- Third reviewed fix round (afternoon): a rep's boxes that an open order still owes can no longer be taken
  off him with an Inventory decrease or damage entry (use a Return on the order); a switched-off product can
  no longer be settled, transferred or added to a rep; a double tap on "More time" gives the time once; the
  owner's "fine is about to start" warning comes again after an order gets more time; a rep suspended from
  his rep profile can no longer sign in; refused sign-ins are recorded in the audit log; failed loads say
  "Couldn't load" with Try again instead of looking empty; finance dates follow the Tanzanian day.
- Sign-in keys: a 30-day renewal key no longer works as a sign-in key (and the reverse); nobody was
  signed out by the change.
